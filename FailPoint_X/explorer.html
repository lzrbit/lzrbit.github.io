Selection and reproduction notes

The point was selected post hoc from frozen evaluations, before these new replays, for an illustrative contrast. Each evaluated actor checkpoint ran once on RTX 5090. All three restored simulator states and initial images are identical. Outcomes and both trained actors’ success steps match the original k = 0 rows. Observation traces differ: original Base-restart ended in a physics fault at step 371; the shown replay reaches 1,200 steps without a physics fault. This example carries no statistical weight.

Videos render every saved execution state through the same simulator and cameras. No policy was rerun to make the videos, and no intermediate frame was generated or interpolated. Each recording includes the restored start and its terminal state, without looping or extra terminal holds. Native resolution is 256 × 256. The original captured keyframes remain available above for interval and endpoint inspection.
